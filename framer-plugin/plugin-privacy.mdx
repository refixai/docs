At Refix, we believe that understanding user behavior shouldn't come at the expense of user privacy. Our Framer plugin is built with a privacy-first approach from the ground up.

## Key Privacy Features

*   **🔒 Cookie-Less Tracking:** Refix does **not** use traditional browser cookies to track users across sessions or sites. This approach respects user privacy, avoids intrusive tracking methods, and helps you comply with regulations like GDPR and ePrivacy Directive without needing complex cookie banners specifically for Refix analytics.

*   **🇪🇺 GDPR Compliant & EU Hosting:** We are committed to complying with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). All our analytics processing and data storage infrastructure are hosted within the European Union (EU), ensuring your website data is handled according to stringent EU privacy standards.

*   **Anonymized Data:** While providing valuable insights into interactions and trends, Refix focuses on aggregated and anonymized data patterns rather than tracking personally identifiable information (PII) through its core autotrack features.

*   **Data Ownership:** You retain ownership of your analytics data.

If you have specific questions about our privacy practices, please refer to our main [Privacy Policy](/privacy/Privacy-Policy) or contact us.
