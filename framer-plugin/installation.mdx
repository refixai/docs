# Installing the Refix Plugin in Framer

You can add the Refix plugin to your Framer project using one of the following methods:

## Method 1: From the Framer Marketplace Website

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Framer Marketplace">
    Go to the [Refix Plugin page](https://framer.com/projects/new?duplicate=refix-analytics-autotrack-plugin) on the Framer website.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Click "Use on Framer"'>
    This will open the plugin directly within Framer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Project">
    Choose the Framer project where you want to install the plugin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install">
    Follow the prompts to complete the installation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Method 2: From Within the Framer Editor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Framer Project">
    Launch Framer and open your target project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Plugins">
    Click "Plugins" in the top menu bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Search for "Refix"'>
    Use the search bar in the Marketplace modal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Refix">
    Click on the Refix plugin in the search results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install">
    Click the "Install" button on the plugin's detail page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once installed, the Refix plugin will be available in your project's Assets or via the Insert menu.
