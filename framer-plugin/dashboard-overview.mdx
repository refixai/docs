## Dashboard Components

The dashboard consists of the following sections:

### 1. Time Period Selector

Located at the top, this dropdown filters dashboard data by "Last 7 days," "Last 30 days," or "Last 60 days."

### 2. Core Metrics: Views & Uniques

Summary cards displaying:

*   **Views:** Total page views within the selected period.
*   **Uniques:** Number of unique visitors during the selected period.
*   **Trend Indicator:** Percentage change compared to the previous period.

### 3. Performance Trend Graph

A line graph visualizing Views or Unique visitors over the selected time period.

### 4. Events Overview

*   **Total Tracked:** The total count of automatically identified interactive elements being tracked.
*   **List Preview:** A preview of recent or frequently interacted-with events.
*   **View All:** Navigates to a detailed view of all tracked events.

### 5. Demographics

Displays audience data categorized by:
*   **Country:** Top countries where visitors are located.
*   **Browser:** Most common web browsers used to access the site.
*   **Device:** Breakdown of access by device type (desktop, mobile, tablet).

### 6. Traffic Sources

Shows how users arrive at the site. Data can be viewed segmented by:
*   **Referrers:** The specific websites linking to your site.
*   **Source:** The origin channel (e.g., Google, Twitter, Direct).
*   **Campaign:** Based on UTM campaign parameters.
*   **Medium:** Based on UTM medium parameters.

This section identifies the channels driving the most traffic to your site.
